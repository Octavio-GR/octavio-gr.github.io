Historical Foundations

The project intentionally adopts timeless principles that have demonstrated persistence across decades or centuries of design, architecture, and human-computer interaction.

Toroide does not imitate products.

Toroide inherits principles.

Foundation 006 — Silent Architecture

The structure does not need a name to govern; the design merely bears witness to it.

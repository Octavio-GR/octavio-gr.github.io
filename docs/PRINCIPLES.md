# PRINCIPLES

These principles are operational.

They are not aspirations.

Every decision inside Toroide should reinforce them.

---

## Principle 001

Reduce uncertainty.

---

## Principle 002

Evidence before opinion.

---

## Principle 003

Structure before decoration.

---

## Principle 004

Systems before pages.

---

## Principle 005

Identity emerges from consistency.

It is never declared.

---

## Principle 006

Silence is communication.

Empty space is part of the architecture.

---

## Principle 007

Documentation is part of the product.

Not a by-product.

---

## Principle 008

Every important claim should eventually become observable.

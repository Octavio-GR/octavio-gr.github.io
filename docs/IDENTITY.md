# Identity

## Who am I

I am the kind of engineer who brings clarity when systems become uncertain.

I don't start with assumptions or quick fixes. I start by understanding how people, processes, and systems interact, separating symptoms from root causes until the problem becomes measurable and actionable.

I believe good engineering is not about having every answer—it's about asking the right questions, reducing complexity, and making decisions that balance technical accuracy with real business impact.

I naturally think in systems rather than isolated tasks. Whether improving a process, investigating an incident, or supporting a critical operation, my goal is always the same: create order from ambiguity, build solutions that others can trust, and leave every system more predictable, resilient, and easier to understand than I found it.

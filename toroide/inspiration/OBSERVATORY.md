# Observatory

Toroide is not a website.

Toroide is an observatory.

People do not enter to consume information.

They enter to discover patterns.

The interface must feel quiet.

The background is not black.

It is depth.

Every accent color should behave like a distant star.

Nothing moves without purpose.

Curiosity is the primary interaction.

Toroide follows museum discipline.

Toroide evokes observatory curiosity.
